# Examples

Use these as voice references, not templates to copy word for word.

## Website copy

Avoid:

> Purpose-built products shaped around real needs, privacy, and thoughtful interaction.

Prefer:

> Apps I built for clients, friends, and myself.

Avoid:

> A transformative platform that seamlessly connects every part of your workflow.

Prefer:

> One place to plan the work and see what is blocked.

## README descriptions

Avoid:

> A robust developer-focused solution for optimizing token utilization across agentic coding workflows.

Prefer:

> See where your coding agent spends its tokens.

Keep project descriptions concrete:

- Private journal that works without an internet connection
- Packing lists based on your trip and the weather
- Party games for one phone

## LinkedIn

Avoid:

> Spearheaded cross-functional initiatives while leveraging deep technical expertise to drive meaningful outcomes.

Prefer factual bullets:

- Led the team that maintained the internal deployment platform.
- Worked with product teams to move services onto it.
- Added checks that caught failed releases before they reached production.

Do not add scale, outcomes, or titles that the source does not support.

## Social posts

Avoid:

> In today's rapidly changing AI landscape, one lesson has become clear: less is more.

Prefer:

> I stopped letting the agent edit files before it explained the plan. The diffs got smaller.

## Messages

Avoid:

> I wanted to reach out regarding the updated onboarding flow and see if you might have some availability to share your thoughts.

Prefer:

> Can you look at the new onboarding flow today? I mainly want to know where it gets confusing.

For operational updates, keep the causal chain and remove the scaffolding.

Avoid:

> The current blocker is deployment approval. The infrastructure team is focused on incident work, and when they are done they can review it. Once we have approval, we can release. So, to answer your question, either we wait for them to finish the incident work, free them from it, or get more people involved.

Prefer:

> Deployment approval is the blocker. The infrastructure team can review it after the incident work, then we can release. So we can wait, free them up sooner, or add more hands.
